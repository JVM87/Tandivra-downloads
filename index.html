Tandivra

Android keyboard and touchpad for your Windows PC, together over Bluetooth.

Download Windows companion 0.5.3 (x64)

Windows 11 x64 and Bluetooth required. Extract the whole ZIP to a folder and run Tandivra.Server.exe. Pair Android with your PC in system Bluetooth settings, open Tandivra on Android and connect. In the local PC panel 127.0.0.1:47832, authorize your device once. Choose Keyboard, Mouse, or Keyboard + mouse. No Wi-Fi or app account needed. The Windows preview executable is not digitally signed.

Android closed testing: email your Google Play email address to seikenjvm@hotmail.com to request access, then join the test once enrolled and the release is available.

Descargas e instalación

Extrae el ZIP completo y ejecuta Tandivra.Server.exe. Empareja Android y PC por Bluetooth, conecta desde Tandivra y autoriza el dispositivo en el panel local del PC. Una autorización permite todos los modos. Para solicitar acceso Android, escribe a seikenjvm@hotmail.com indicando el correo de tu cuenta Google Play.
